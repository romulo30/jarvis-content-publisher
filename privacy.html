Privacy Policy

Jarvis Content Publisher is a personal desktop application designed to help authorized users prepare, manage, schedule and publish their own video content to social media platforms.

Authentication

The application may use OAuth authorization provided by supported platforms. Users must explicitly authorize their own accounts before publishing functionality can be used.

Information Used

The application may process information necessary to authenticate the user, identify authorized accounts, prepare content and perform publishing operations requested by the user.

Local Data

Application configuration, publishing jobs, authorization information and content-processing information may be stored locally on the user's computer.

Content Publishing

Jarvis Content Publisher is intended to publish content only to accounts authorized by the user. Authorization can be revoked through the relevant platform when supported.

Third-Party Services

The application may interact with third-party platforms such as TikTok, YouTube and Facebook through their respective APIs and authorization systems. Use of those services is also subject to their own policies.

Contact

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy may be directed to the application owner through the contact information associated with the application.
