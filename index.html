Jarvis Content Publisher

Manage and publish your video content with Jarvis.

Jarvis Content Publisher is a desktop application designed to help users prepare, organize, manage and publish their own video content to authorized social media accounts.

What is Jarvis?

Jarvis helps creators manage their video publishing workflow from preparation to publication. Users maintain control over their accounts and explicitly authorize supported social media integrations.

Features

Content Management

Organize and manage video content before publication.

Video Preparation

Prepare video content and publishing information in one workflow.

Social Publishing

Publish content to supported social platforms after account authorization.

Secure Authorization

Jarvis uses official authorization mechanisms such as OAuth and does not access accounts without user permission.

TikTok Integration

Jarvis integrates with TikTok through official TikTok authorization and content publishing APIs. The user connects and authorizes their own TikTok account before Jarvis can perform any publishing action.


Jarvis does not publish content to TikTok accounts without explicit authorization from the account owner.
